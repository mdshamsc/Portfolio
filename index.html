> work 01/03

Cut unnecessary emails and calls by 15%

Client
Shifl Inc.
Project
Customer portal
Result
15% fewer unnecessary emails and calls

Shifl's customers tracked shipments through calls and emails. I led the design of a customer portal that puts shipment updates and key services in one place.

Customers can now see where their cargo is without asking. Fewer manual handoffs meant fewer errors, and unnecessary emails and calls dropped by 15%.
